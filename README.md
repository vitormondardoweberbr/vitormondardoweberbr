## 👤 Professional Profile

**Systems Developer and Data Analyst** holding a degree in **Information Systems**, working at the intersection of **Full Stack Software Engineering**, **Systems Architecture**, and **Data Engineering & Analytics**.

Focused on building scalable, maintainable, data-driven enterprise applications by combining sound architectural practices, process automation, continuous quality in CI/CD, and the strategic application of Artificial Intelligence.

---

## 💼 Experience & Technical Expertise

* **Full Stack Systems Development:** building and evolving an enterprise customer service platform integrated with **WhatsApp (Meta Cloud API)**, centralizing ticket management, technician scheduling, spare parts control, and real-time notifications.
* **Architecture & Back-end:** development with **Node.js**, **TypeScript**, and **NestJS** structured as a modular monolith with **Clean Architecture** and *bounded contexts*, persistence in **PostgreSQL** using **TypeORM**, and asynchronous messaging via **RabbitMQ**.
* **Front-end:** building modern, responsive web interfaces with **React**, **Vite**, and **Next.js**.
* **Data & Analytics:** data modeling and analysis with **PostgreSQL**, **Python**, **Pandas**, and **Scikit-Learn**, turning operational data into KPIs and actionable insights for decision-making.
* **Cloud, Infrastructure & Quality:** environment containerization with **Docker**, cloud infrastructure on **Oracle Cloud**, **Azure**, and **Terraform**, **CI/CD pipelines in GitHub Actions** (lint, typecheck, tests, and build), and architectural decision documentation via **ADRs** (*Architecture Decision Records*).
* **Applied AI & Automation:** applying **Claude**, **OpenAI Codex**, **Ollama**, *Prompt Engineering*, and **AI Agents** for intelligent automation and engineering productivity gains.
